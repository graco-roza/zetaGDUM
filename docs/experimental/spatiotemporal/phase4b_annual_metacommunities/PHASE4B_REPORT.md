# Phase IV-B · Continuous annual metacommunities

## Main result

Annual paths revealed compositional reorganisation that endpoint attribution concealed. In the BioTIME archive illustration, mean spatial Sørensen dissimilarity across all 21 trap pairs fell from **0.57113** in 2004 to **0.55353** in 2013. Five annual transitions homogenised the panel and four differentiated it. Local temporal Sørensen averaged **0.244** and ranged from **0.227 to 0.271**. In 2009–2010, local temporal Sørensen was **0.227** while pairwise spatial dissimilarity changed by **−0.00202**.

The illustration uses seven traps, below the 20-site exploratory minimum. No Finnish panel met the minimum among data that could be audited. The empirical numbers describe these traps and surveys. Continuous simulations provide controlled validation. Regional empirical validation remains open.

## Data provenance and coverage

The [BioTIME study 604 catalogue](https://biotime.st-andrews.ac.uk/selectStudy.php?study=604) and [original B2SHARE workbook](https://doi.org/10.23728/b2share.c9399a3ccb61450aaf58042b88a142fd) describe nightly mercury-vapour light traps, typically operated April–October and usually emptied weekly. BioTIME lists ODC-By. Cite BioTIME and the original archive for reuse. The download parses **47,343 records, 282 distinct trap-years and 649 unique taxon names**, compared with 651 on the catalogue page. All 14 trap codes are present in both sources, and each has a single coordinate pair in BioTIME. The illustrated panel contains **593 taxa**. There were 34 duplicate trap-year-taxon keys with one extra record each. Any positive record establishes recorded presence. The optional catch sensitivity sums the rows. The two taxon inventories are saved separately. BioTIME accepted names are retained as archived. Discordant names between the original and harmonized deposits were not pooled.

Neither BioTIME nor the workbook provides weekly dates or trap nights. Annual totals ranged from 129 to 221,697 moths per recorded trap-year, reflecting both ecological variation and unknown variation in effort. Trap duration, exact seasonal coverage and protocol changes at individual traps could not be evaluated. The workbook's effort declaration includes a malformed range for trap 1310. Nine trap-years have positive BioTIME observations without an unambiguous matching workbook coverage declaration. In the archive, missing site-years remain missing. A zero inside an observed community means no positive record for that taxon. The archive has no explicitly empty annual communities.

Candidate panels were ranked using archive coverage alone. The largest panel of at least ten consecutive surveys was seven traps in 2004–2013. The largest panel of at least 15 years was five traps in 1993–2007. [Coverage candidates](results/balanced_panel_candidates.csv), [membership](results/balanced_panel_membership.csv), and [effort](results/sampling_effort.csv) give all intervals and exclusions.

The [Finnish breeding bird Dryad record](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.bzkh189br) describes annual breeding-season route visits and inclusion of waterbirds in Finland from 2006. Public coordinates are coarsened. Stable route identifiers, protocol flags and coverage for 2006–2016 require the actual observations. The linked Zenodo deposit contains analysis scripts only. The [Finnish Lepidoptera Dryad record](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.hhmgqnkgh) describes 70 traps in its moth trend subset and 101 butterfly transects visited from 1999 to 2017. These archive totals do not establish balanced panels. Both Dryad records are CC0. Their file manifests returned lengths and SHA-256 values. Public file downloads returned HTTP 403 and API downloads HTTP 401 for the required data and READMEs. Browser attempts did not yield verified files. The larger moth archive, birds, and butterfly fallback remain unaudited at the record level. See the [download manifest](results/download_manifest.csv).

Near the dissimilarity ceiling, declining magnitudes cannot be used to rank ecological process importance.

The two aligned windows start in 2004 and 2009. The lag is five years at each aligned position. Window duration ranges from zero to four years as m goes from one to five. Exact initial sharing was **94.71 taxa** at (2,1), **46.95** at (2,5) and **4.86** at (6,5). Effective contributing taxon counts were **199.65**, **111.11** and **7.23**. Initial temporal and spatial retention at (6,5) were **0.7083** and **0.6071**, respectively. At that order all empirical sets had defined incidence dissimilarity and nonzero initial sharing, and 86% changed in shared-species temporal Sørensen.

At spatial order 2, expansion contributed **−0.04636** with one survey per window and **−0.04398** with five. Balanced redistribution shifted from **+0.00474** to **−0.00193**. There is only one full five-year window alignment in this panel. Spatial occupancy, temporal persistence and detection cannot be isolated by contrasting orders alone. In the representative detection trajectory, 99.98% of sampled final rectangles at (6,5) had zero shared taxa on average. Only 0.72% supported shared temporal Sørensen across all coalitions. The [response-specific diagnostics](results/diagnostics.csv) distinguish undefined responses, saturation and small changes on valid support.

## Known-process trajectories

Eight scenarios each used 24 independent primary panels of 60 sites, 200 taxa and 30 annual surveys, plus one smaller 30-site, 120-taxon, 15-year panel per scenario. Seeds are matched across compatible scenarios. States carry forward changes from one year to the next. Scenarios include progressive gains and losses, opposing processes, a whole-community permutation, autocorrelated magnitudes of balanced reshuffling, reversal, missed detections on a static latent panel, and a static null.

For pure and opposing trajectories, the exact mean raw-zeta contribution is independently known from the change in each manipulated taxon's occupancy. Site-label permutations preserve each complete-combination spatial response even when local histories change. The permutation panels had mean annual local Sørensen **0.34514** and exact pairwise spatial change zero to floating-point precision. Reversal panels returned to their starting communities after annual gains and losses. The static latent panel with missed detections produced mean observed annual local Sørensen **0.22544**, versus **0** for the latent communities. AR(1) shocks yielded mean lag-one correlation **0.54348** in redistribution event counts.

The [replicate results](results/simulation_ecological.csv), [sampled versus exact contrasts](results/simulation_summary.csv), [Monte Carlo calibration](results/simulation_calibration.csv) and [order support](results/simulation_order_support.csv) provide the full numerical results. Calibration uses finite-panel truth and targets the variability of sampled combinations. With 192 independently drawn spatial sets per non-exhaustive order held fixed across years, nominal 95% Monte Carlo intervals covered 79–100% of exact nonzero cumulative contrasts across configurations at orders 2–6. RMSE ranged from 0.013 to 0.266 taxa in mean shared richness. For the opposing scenario at order 2, contraction coverage was 22/24 (exact binomial interval 0.730–0.990) and expansion coverage was 23/24 (0.789–0.999). Valid ecological intervals across panels were not established. The smaller panels are a dimensional sensitivity.

## Mathematics and uncertainty

Eight coalitions of expansion, contraction and balanced redistribution are evaluated within every admissible allocation. Classification occurs across the complete regional panel for each aligned survey position. Allocations are shared across every response and spatial order in a contrast. Undefined denominators invalidate the response for that set across all coalitions and draws. For example, two empty shared compositions make temporal Sørensen undefined. Zero sharing alone does not make spatial Sørensen undefined when mean richness is positive. Cumulative support is intersected over the endpoint and all annual transitions.

The [65 mathematical checks](results/mathematical_validation.csv) include agreement with the four-block dummy case, the native package responses, analytical shared richness, empty-assemblage support and pure redistribution. Maximum discrepancy was **2.14 × 10⁻¹⁴**. Maximum empirical Shapley efficiency error was **8.0 × 10⁻¹⁵**.

Combination-sampling error was zero for the seven-trap illustration because every spatial combination was evaluated. The primary analysis used 64 uniform draws where regional classification was ambiguous. The largest annual pairwise incidence allocation standard error of a process mean was **0.000105**. The maximum across representative orders and contrasts fell from **0.000085** at 32 draws to **0.000024** at 512. Each draw is shared across all sets. Deleting one of four latitude blocks while retaining original regional allocation yielded annual pairwise spatial sensitivity scales of **0.00190–0.01693**. Some higher-order deletions leave no sets. Three contiguous three-transition blocks give descriptive temporal sensitivity. The small panel and single five-year alignment cannot calibrate ecological uncertainty or crossed spatial–temporal inference.

Negative contributions to normalized dissimilarity indicate homogenisation, and positive contributions indicate differentiation. Raw-zeta similarity uses the reverse sign interpretation. Directional percentages divide by within-direction magnitudes before cancellation. Balanced redistribution describes concurrent changes in occupied sites. Individual movement and causal effects require other evidence.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, run the numbered scripts via [run_all.sh](scripts/run_all.sh). Dependencies are a C++ compiler, R, Rcpp, data.table, digest, jsonlite, readxl, ggplot2, patchwork, ragg, and the repository version of zetaGDUM. The environment variable ST4B_SIM_REPLICATES sets the number of primary simulations per scenario. Released results use the default 24. Raw files and compiled caches stay local. The download script can verify manually retrieved Dryad files against the archive metadata. Recovered archives still require a protocol and coverage audit before panel selection. The [ten figures](figures/) are supplied as PDF and PNG.
