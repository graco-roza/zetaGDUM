# Swedish lake data provenance and monitoring audit

**Source.** Johnson, Goedkoop and Lau (2025), Global Change Biology, and version 1 of the Swedish National Data Service dataset `2025-136`, published under CC0 1.0. The article and its companion supplement are open under CC BY 4.0 and credited here to the authors. The biological archive was the sole source of compositional observations. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.70274) · [Catalogue and license](https://researchdata.se/en/catalogue/dataset/2025-136) · [Dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.5878/03t1-sv66)

`scripts/01_download.R` first retrieves the catalogue ZIP to pin the publisher's SHA-256 list, then obtains individual API files and uses the official ZIP as fallback if an individual request or hash check fails. The initial download encountered a DNS restriction in the workspace sandbox, then a 404 on a physicochemical path whose filename was spelled differently in the official archive. The catalogue ZIP supplied the missing files and checksum list. The archived physicochemical file is literally named `01_Physochemical.tsv`. Direct and fallback files were checked against publisher hashes, parsed, and checked for HTML or JSON responses. `ST4_REFRESH=1` forces a reproducible refetch. `ST4_CATALOGUE_ZIP` reuses an already downloaded ZIP. The physicochemical and climate data are present for archival reproducibility and are outside the analysis. [Archive manifest](data/raw/manifest.csv) · [Catalogue ZIP](https://api.researchdata.se/dataset/2025-136/1/file/zip)

**Design and protocol.** The lake identifier is `EU_ID`. Lake-year keys are unique in each taxon and metric table. The article and archive metadata describe standardized SWEDAC-accredited protocols across the study. Phytoplankton observations are August epilimnetic tube samples from open mid-lake water, with taxon biovolumes derived from geometric measurements. Littoral macroinvertebrates were sampled mainly October–November by five 20-second one-metre kick samples with 0.5 mm mesh in shallow stony habitat. Profundal invertebrates were sampled by five standardized Ekman grabs over the deepest area, with the same mesh and abundance expressed per square metre. The publication identifies limits to species-level resolution for Oligochaeta and some chironomids. The archive lacks replicate effort fields and year-specific method-change flags. The comparability assumption rests on the documented design and methods. [Archive metadata](data/raw/Metadata.txt) · [Published protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12146949/#gcb70274-sec-0005)

**Map and spatial blocks.** The Europe PMC supplemental archive contains the published Table S1 DOCX. Its first table supplies the coordinates. A normalized name join links Table S1 to the archive, using a recorded Rännöbodsjön alias and the identifier's RT90 location code to distinguish two pairs of homonymous lakes. The map uses the *published* coordinates. The reproduced Table S1 has a caption mentioning ecoregions and no usable lake-level ecoregion field. The paper discusses six ecoregions, but membership is left unassigned in the analysis. Ecological uncertainty therefore uses six contiguous latitude-rank blocks formed over the 110 named sites. They are reproducible geographic deletion units, not ecoregions. [Supplement](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12146949/supplementaryFiles) · [Coordinate join](data/processed/lake_geography.csv)

**Presence and missingness.** Coverage identifies nonempty records with one finite full taxon row. There are no verified all-zero communities. Littoral has 18 records with missing taxon values and no matching metric row. Profundal has 76 all-zero rows. Seventy-five have missing abundance and richness metrics, and one is inconsistent with the positive archived metric. These are kept in the audit and excluded from defined relative-composition panels. Undocumented or unsampled lake-years have no filled community values. Archived metric richness occasionally differs from the number of positive taxon columns. The explicit taxon matrix defines incidence throughout. [Archive audit](results/archive_audit.csv) · [Status by lake and year](results/lake_year_status.csv)

**Composition and taxonomy.** Square the archived Hellinger values and close each nonempty row to one. Square-sum deviations are below \(2.6\times10^{-9}\) in all three datasets. We retain rare taxa, preserve ambiguous named groups, and merge only transparent punctuation and equivalent group-abbreviation variants. Unverified biological synonyms remain separate. The reproducible genus sensitivity pools names sharing a genus token, preserves recognized higher-rank categories and keeps mixed-genus complexes as separate labels. It is a resolution sensitivity with conservative exceptions, rather than an external taxonomic revision. The mapping records the original label, harmonized label and genus sensitivity label. [Mapping table](data/processed/taxonomy_mapping.csv)

**Panels.** The declared exact years found too few consistently observed lakes for a common three-assemblage comparison under a coverage floor of 30. The earliest common five-year baseline satisfying that floor was 1995–1999, and the target remained 2018–2022. Each matched survey position is 23 years apart. A fixed annual 1995–2022 panel retains at least 30 lakes in each assemblage, the earliest common start meeting that floor through 2022. The original designs are explicitly audited and available as sensitivity analyses for supported taxa. Panel membership is determined entirely by available nonempty surveys.
